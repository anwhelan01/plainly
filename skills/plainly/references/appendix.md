# Anti-slop appendix

Google never listed Claude-lish. These tells are the extra layer. Delete them
on sight unless they are a quotation or a required term.

## Openers

The model clears its throat before it speaks. Start with the answer.

- I'd be happy to
- Certainly!
- Absolutely!
- Of course!
- Sure thing!
- Great question
- As an AI
- I hope this helps
- Without further ado
- Let's get started
- Let's dive in / delve / explore / take a look / break it down / unpack
- delve into
- deep dive
- take a closer look at

## Wrappers

If it's important, the sentence already is. Cut the preface.

- It's important to note
- It's worth noting
- It should be noted
- Needless to say
- It goes without saying
- As previously mentioned
- Rest assured

## Brochure

Press-release English. These words rarely survive a fact check. Say what
actually happens.

- cutting-edge, groundbreaking, game-changer
- state-of-the-art, next-generation, best-in-class, world-class
- seamless, robust
- empower, unleash, unlock the potential
- elevate your, transform the way
- foster, harness
- multifaceted, pivotal
- tapestry, landscape of
- underscores the importance
- revolutionize, supercharge
- holistic, synergy, paradigm
- in today's rapidly evolving
- plays a vital role
- comprehensive suite
- invaluable
- moving forward, going forward
- at a high level
- when it comes to

## Hedges

If they should do it, tell them.

- feel free to
- don't hesitate to
- you may want to
- you might want to
- it is recommended that you

## False ease

These words shame the reader when the step isn't obvious. Google flags them too.

- simply
- easily
- it's easy to
- just click / just run
- please note
- please click
- please make sure

## Padding

Three words that mean one word, or a forecast of the page you already opened.

- in order to → to
- due to the fact that → because
- in the event that → if
- for the purpose of → to / for
- at this point in time → now
- leverage / utilize / facilitate → use
- in this guide / this document will / we will walk you through → start the task
